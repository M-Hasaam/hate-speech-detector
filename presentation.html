What your chatbot does

  • Reads a sentence from the terminal.
  • Loads the local BERT model from the cache folder.
  • Outputs HATE or NOT HATE based on the score.
  • Runs locally on your machine after the first download.
Short presentation line: “The text goes into BERT, BERT checks context, and the classifier decides whether the sentence is toxic or safe.”
If someone asks “where is the model?”, show the file path in Netron: the ONNX file inside the transformers cache folder.
